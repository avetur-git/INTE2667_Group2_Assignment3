Electronic Voting Platform

Base build for federal elections: enrolment, House of Representatives and Senate voting, counting and results. Security solutions are added on top of this base.

Create voter account Login
Voters

Create an account, enrol (or check your status), update your address, then vote online, at a polling station terminal, or at a diplomatic mission if overseas.

Staff

AEC officers register citizens. Commissioner's delegates enter candidates and handle court-ordered exclusions. Election admins run the election, terminals and the count.

Results

House seats are counted with preferential voting. Senate seats use a quota with surplus transfers. Results can be exported for external systems.
